Privacy & Cookie Notice

How we look after your information at March for Jesus Dublin.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This notice explains what personal information the March for Jesus Dublin website (marchforjesus.ie) collects, how we use it, and how we use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising measurement. The event is organised by Christian Voice Ireland.

We process personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. 336 of 2011).

1. Information we collect

When you sign up for updates or contact us

Our sign-up form collects the details you provide, which may include:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • County
  • Church or organisation (optional)
  • Whether you have attended a March for Jesus before
  • Your consent to receive communications

We use this to send you news and updates about March for Jesus Dublin. Email delivery is handled by our email provider, MailerLite. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

When you browse the site

If you accept analytics and advertising cookies, we and our providers collect standard technical information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and interactions such as completing the sign-up form. We do not send your name, email, phone, church or other form contents to our analytics or advertising providers.

2. Cookies & tracking

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept non-essential cookies. These load only after you click “Accept”. If you choose “Reject”, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

We use Google Consent Mode so Google tags respect your choice. Meta and TikTok tags are not loaded at all unless you consent.

3. Legal basis

  • Consent — for marketing emails, and for analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Legitimate interests — for responding to enquiries you send us and for the secure operation of the website.

4. Sharing your information

We share personal data only with providers who help us run the event and this website, including:

  • MailerLite — email communications
  • Google, Meta and TikTok — analytics and advertising measurement (only with your consent)
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and delivery

We do not sell your personal data. Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up details for as long as you wish to receive updates, and remove them if you unsubscribe or ask us to. Analytics data is retained according to our providers’ standard retention periods.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. You can also withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, contact us below. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

7. Contact

Questions about this notice or your data? Email information@marchforjesus.ie.

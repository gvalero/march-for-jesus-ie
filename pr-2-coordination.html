March for Jesus Dublin Confirms Safe Event Planning with An Garda Síochána and Dublin City Council

Organisers confirm coordination with police and council to ensure March for Jesus Dublin and other 26 September city events proceed safely with no conflicts.

March for Jesus Ireland confirms that comprehensive planning has been undertaken with Dublin City Council and An Garda Síochána to ensure March for Jesus Dublin and concurrent city events on 26 September proceed safely and without disruption.

"Coordination is key to a successful day for everyone," said John Ahern, organiser of March for Jesus Dublin. "We've worked with Dublin City Council, An Garda Síochána, and other stakeholders to ensure all events on 26 September can proceed smoothly. An Garda Síochána has been instrumental in liaising between all event organisers to map out timing, routes, and safety measures. We're confident in the planning that's been put in place."

Community Commitment:

March for Jesus Dublin is explicitly non-political and welcomes participation from all churches, denominations, and individuals regardless of background. The event is characterised by worship, prayer, and positive public testimony to Christian faith.

Comprehensive planning with Dublin City Council and An Garda Síochána has ensured safe management of all 26 September events. An Garda Síochána has coordinated with relevant city-centre stakeholders to confirm routes, manage traffic, prevent crowd overlap, and ensure public safety. Key measures in place:

• Coordinated routes and timing managed by An Garda Síochána and council
• Staggered event schedules confirmed through An Garda Síochána liaison
• Trained stewards at March for Jesus Dublin
• Qualified paramedics on-site for health and safety
• Gardaí traffic management and coordination through 26 September

The march will begin from a Dublin city-centre venue (TBC) at 2:00 PM on 26 September and proceed along the march route through Dublin city centre (route TBC) for a concluding time of worship and prayer from 3:00–5:00 PM at a Dublin city-centre venue (TBC). All participants are encouraged to conduct themselves in a spirit of peace and respect.

"This is a significant event for the city," John concluded. "We're grateful for An Garda Síochána's professionalism in bringing all parties together. Dublin is big enough for everyone to gather peacefully and celebrate what matters to them. That's what the planning shows—respectful coexistence."

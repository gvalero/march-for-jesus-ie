March for Jesus Dublin Advance Traffic Notice: Careful Planning to Minimise Disruption on 26 September 2026

March for Jesus Ireland apologises in advance for temporary traffic disruption in central Dublin on Saturday 26 September as thousands gather to celebrate faith and unity.

The annual March for Jesus Dublin will draw an estimated 16,000–20,000 participants from churches across Ireland and beyond. The organisers acknowledge the impact on local traffic flow and wish to thank the public, local businesses, and Dublin City Council for their patience and cooperation.

"We recognise that a public gathering of this scale creates temporary challenges for traffic and local residents," said John Ahern, organiser of March for Jesus Dublin. "Our team has worked carefully with council partners and traffic authorities to minimise disruption. We are grateful for the professionalism and support we have received."

Event Details:

• Date: Saturday 26 September 2026
• Start: 10:00 AM gathering at Dublin city-centre venue (TBC)
• March departure: 2:00 PM from Dublin city-centre venue (TBC)
• Route: the march route through Dublin city centre (route TBC)
• Conclusion: 3:00–5:00 PM at Dublin city-centre venue (TBC)

The march is family-friendly, non-political, and open to all ages and denominations. Parking, access, route details and FAQs are TBC and will be available at marchforjesus.ie.

March for Jesus Dublin is organised by March for Jesus Ireland in partnership with churches across the region. The event celebrates Christian faith through worship, prayer, and public testimony to exalt the Name of Jesus Christ in the heart of Dublin.

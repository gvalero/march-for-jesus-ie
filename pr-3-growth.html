March for Jesus Dublin 2026 Expected to Draw 16,000–20,000 Attendees; John Ahern Leads Growing Movement

March for Jesus Dublin is on track for continued momentum, with organisers expecting 16,000–20,000 participants on Saturday 26 September 2026, following the success of 25,000 attendees in Dublin and 8,000+ in Belfast in 2025.

March for Jesus Ireland has announced that this year's March for Jesus Dublin is expected to attract a strong city-centre turnout following the major Dublin gathering last year. The growing momentum reflects increased church involvement, broader community participation, and a groundswell of support for public Christian witness across Ireland.

John Ahern, organiser of March for Jesus Dublin and coordinator of March for Jesus across Europe, said: "Last year we brought together 25,000 people in Dublin and over 8,000 in Belfast. The response has been extraordinary. This year, we're preparing for significant growth as more churches, communities, and individuals recognise the power of unified Christian public testimony."

"March for Jesus is about exalting the Name of Jesus Christ in the heart of our cities. It's non-political, family-friendly, and rooted in worship, prayer, and hope. As we face unprecedented spiritual and social challenges, Christians from every denomination are uniting to declare our faith publicly and peacefully. The movement is growing across Europe and the world—Dublin is part of a much larger story," John explained.

2026 Expansion:

• Expected attendance: 16,000–20,000 (following 25,000 in Dublin in 2025)
• European coordination: John Ahern leads March for Jesus across multiple European nations
• Multi-denominational participation: Churches and Christian groups from across Ireland
• Family-friendly focus: Entire families, youth groups, and individuals welcome
